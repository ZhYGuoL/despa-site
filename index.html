02

How it works

  1. 1

    Add the documents

    Drop in the files of one shipment: PDF, Excel, Word or scans. A shipment can have several invoices and packing lists.

  2. 2

    Despa compares them

    Every value is compared across documents, with your company's rules and with the Chile–China FTA. What needs your decision comes first.

  3. 3

    Ask for the fix

    One message per supplier, in Chinese, Spanish or English, with each value to change and the documents to send again.

05

What it checks

Each importer sets their rules on one screen, with no code.

RuleWhat it compares
ConsigneeName and address, against the text your company requires, on every document.
Shipper and notify partyThat they match on the invoice, packing list and bill of lading.
Invoice numberThe same number on every document that cites it.
Gross weightThe packing list total against the invoice and the bill of lading.
GoodsThe description of each item on every document, including the Form F.
Ports and termsPort of loading, trade terms, and freight on the bill of lading.
Amount in wordsThat the total written in words is the invoice total.
Form FIssuer, HS codes against the invoice, validity and origin criterion under the Chile–China FTA.
