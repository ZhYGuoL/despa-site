06

What it checks

Each importer sets their rules on one screen, with no code.

  1. 01

    Consignee

    Name and address, against the text your company requires, on every document.

  2. 02

    Shipper and notify party

    That they match on the invoice, packing list and bill of lading.

  3. 03

    Invoice number

    The same number on every document that cites it.

  4. 04

    Gross weight

    The packing list total against the invoice and the bill of lading.

  5. 05

    Goods

    The description of each item on every document, including the Form F.

  6. 06

    Ports and terms

    Port of loading, trade terms, and freight on the bill of lading.

  7. 07

    Amount in words

    That the total written in words is the invoice total.

  8. 08

    Form F

    Issuer, HS codes against the invoice, validity and origin criterion under the Chile–China FTA.
